FAQs

Emergency Locksmith FAQs

Are your locksmiths available 24/7?
Yes. Our emergency locksmith service operates 24 hours a day, including weekends and bank holidays.
How quickly can a locksmith arrive?
Arrival times depend on your location, traffic and locksmith availability. Contact us for an estimated arrival time.
Can you open my door without damaging it?
We prioritise non-destructive entry where practical; the method depends on the lock type and circumstances.
How much does an emergency locksmith cost?
Prices depend on the lock type, work required, time of attendance and any replacement parts. Contact us for specifics.
Can you replace my locks immediately?
We can replace many common lock types during an emergency appointment, subject to stock availability.
Do you repair uPVC door locks?
Yes. We assist with uPVC and composite door locking problems, including faulty cylinders and multipoint locking.
What if my key has snapped in the lock?
We assess snapped-key situations and determine whether the key can be extracted or further work is required.
Do you help businesses?
Yes. We provide locksmith services for residential and commercial properties.
