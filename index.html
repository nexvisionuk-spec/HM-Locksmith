Common questions answered

Quick answers to common questions about our services, response times, and how we work.

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

We offer a 24/7 emergency service with a target response time of around 30 minutes across London. Our team is always ready to assist you when you need urgent help.

Do you offer fixed pricing?

Yes, we believe in transparent and fixed pricing. Before starting any work, we clearly explain the cost so there are no hidden surprises.

What types of projects do you handle?

We take on commercial, home, and public builds, giving each client a plan made for their goals and site needs.

Do you handle insurance-related work?

Yes, we carry out insurance repairs and reinstatement works. We can assist with assessments, repairs, and restoring your property after damage.
