About H&M

A London family firm built on fast response, honest pricing and workmanship that lasts.

Our Story

Looking after London since 2009

H&M Locksmith & Building Services Ltd began as a small team of locksmiths determined to give Londoners a service they could actually rely on — someone who answers the phone at 3am, turns up fast, and charges a fair, agreed price.

Over 15 years later we've grown into two specialist divisions covering the full range of property needs: H&M Locksmith & Security for locks, keys and security, and H&M Domestic & Building for electrics, plumbing, refurbishment and maintenance. Many of our engineers began their careers as apprentices with us and have stayed ever since.

Whether you're a homeowner, landlord, letting agent or business, you get one trusted team from the first call to the final tidy-up.
