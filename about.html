Who We Are

A team built on trust, skill & reliability

H&M Group is a trusted provider of property services across London, looking after homeowners, landlords and businesses since 2009. Our multi-trade specialists handle everything from urgent emergency repairs to large-scale projects — all under one roof.

We're focused on giving you a smooth, stress-free experience: we take the time to understand exactly what you need, respond quickly, and finish every job to a high standard. From a single lock change to a full refurbishment, you deal with one reliable team from the first call to the final tidy-up.

Our two specialist divisions — H&M Locksmith & Security and H&M Domestic & Building — mean one trusted company can take care of your whole property.
